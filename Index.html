How to read this prototype

Aeris is a desktop tool for an equity research team on an earnings morning, when many companies report results before the market opens. This version walks through it with notes on every new idea.

linear_scaleTop: the user journey. Jump to any scene.
desktop_windowsLeft: the prototype. A glowing outline shows where to click next.
menu_bookRight: the story behind each idea, in a few lines.

All companies, people and numbers are made up for illustration.
